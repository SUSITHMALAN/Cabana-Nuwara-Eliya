Bookings & Reservations

Reservations

You can book directly via our online booking form, WhatsApp us at +94 70 173 1443, or email us at eurynaturecabana26@gmail.com. We always recommend booking directly with us for the best rates.
Cancellations made more than 48 hours before check-in are eligible for a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours may forfeit the deposit. Please contact us via WhatsApp as early as possible if your plans change.
We welcome both single-night and multi-night bookings. There is no minimum stay requirement, though we recommend at least 2 nights to fully enjoy the highland experience and nearby attractions.
We accept bank transfers and cash payments at the property. Please contact us to confirm the payment details when you make your booking. A deposit may be required to secure your reservation.
Yes! We have two cabanas — the Main Cabana (sleeps up to 9) and the Little Eury Nature Cabana (sleeps up to 4). Just mention your preference when booking and we will do our best to accommodate you.
Check-in & Check-out

Your Arrival

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. If you need early check-in or late check-out, please contact us in advance and we will try to accommodate based on availability.
We are located 12 km from Nuwara Eliya and 4 km from Ambewela Station. The easiest way is by car or tuk-tuk from Nuwara Eliya. We will send you a Google Maps link with directions when your booking is confirmed.
Yes, we have secure on-site parking available at no extra charge for all guests. The parking area is monitored and secure throughout your stay.
Amenities & Facilities

At the Cabana

The main cabana includes 3 bedrooms, a full kitchen, dining area, living room, BBQ machine, washroom with hot water, TV, carrom board & games, and safe parking. The Little Cabana includes twin beds, veranda, mountain view, TV, and hot water.
Yes, the main cabana kitchen is fully equipped with cooking utensils, gas stove, refrigerator, and basic condiments. You can bring your own groceries or we can arrange meals for you on request.
Yes, high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the property. The highland location can occasionally cause brief interruptions but connectivity is generally reliable.
We are a pet-free property to ensure comfort and cleanliness for all guests. We appreciate your understanding.
Eury Nature Cabana is a non-smoking property. Smoking is not permitted inside any cabana or common indoor areas. Please respect this policy during your stay.
Dining

Food & Meals

Yes! We offer freshly prepared Sri Lankan meals — breakfast (7–8:30 AM), lunch (12–2 PM), and dinner (6–9 PM). All meals are made to order and must be requested in advance. See our full menu.
Please let us know your dietary needs when booking (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, allergies). We will do our best to accommodate all requirements with fresh, locally sourced ingredients.
